Then "Where it comes from" section, we got Three valleys i.e. three harvests
1) Chamba & Bharmour - 2,000 – 2,900 m · Ravi Basin
Bharmour rajma is a mountain bean — small, thin-skinned, and grown on plots too steep for a tractor. It takes a long season and a cold night to build the skin that lets it hold shape through a slow braise.
2) Kullu Valley - 1,800 – 3,200 m · Beas Basin
Deodar and blue-pine forest on the Beas gives the valley its damp, mineral floor. After the snow retreats in March, guchhi fruits along the leaf litter for a window of barely six weeks. Nobody farms it — the same families walk the same slopes their grandparents did.
3) Shimla Hills 1,400 – 2,400 m · Mid-Himalaya
Terraced fields cut into the oak belt above Shimla. The red rice here is grown on rain and snowmelt alone, in soil that has never been levelled flat. Cool nights slow the grain and set the anthocyanin that gives it its colour.
